I started this blog after a missed connection in Ljubljana turned into the best three days of a holiday. Since then I plan less and write more down.

## How I travel

- By train, bus and ferry wherever one exists. I have not flown inside Europe since 2017.
- Out of season, because prices drop and people have time to talk.
- With one rucksack of seven kilos, and a notebook that weighs more than it should.

> The detour is usually the part I remember.

## What every post tells you

How long it took, how I got there and what I spent, receipts included. Nobody pays me to go anywhere; if that ever changes, the post will say so in its first line.
