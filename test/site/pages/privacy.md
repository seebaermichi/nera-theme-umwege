## Server log files

When you visit this site, the web server stores your IP address, the time and the page requested in log files (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). They are deleted after seven days.

## Cookies and device storage

This site stores nothing on your device and reads nothing from it, so no consent under § 25 TDDDG is needed. Its fonts are served from this site; it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other hosts.
