The night train from Munich reaches Bolzano at ten to six, which is early enough to watch the station café open and late enough to feel you have earned the first espresso. From there the Dolomites are a network of blue buses that run all year, cost less than a sandwich and are driven by men who greet every passenger by name.

I had nine days, a rucksack and a rule: no taxis, no rental car, no lifts I could walk instead.

## The valley nobody stops in

Between Bolzano and Cortina the bus changes in Dobbiaco. Most people wait twenty minutes on the platform. I stayed two nights, because the guesthouse opposite had a wood stove and [a landlady who rents out snowshoes](/posts/south-tyrol-wrong-season.html) for the price of a coffee. I did not need them yet: above the village the meadows were still green, and I walked up to the hut in a T-shirt. The snow came three days later, overnight, all at once.

![A wooden hut on a green meadow below a rock massif](/images/hardial-aujla-0IZdJRSKvGI-unsplash.jpg) ![Jagged peaks dusted with snow catching the first orange light](/images/stefano-bazzoli-6bav58U0kIU-unsplash.jpg)

The hut above Dobbiaco on day two, green as June, and the Cristallo group three days later at minus eleven degrees. The village in the picture at the top is the last one before the pass, on day six.

> **Worth knowing** The guest card you get at any guesthouse makes every regional bus free. Ask for it at check-in; nobody offers it.

## What I would do differently

Come a week earlier for the larches or three weeks later for proper winter. The changeover is beautiful, but half the huts are shut. Skip the famous lake, which in November is a car park with a view. And book the night train back before leaving home, not from a bus shelter with one bar of signal.

## The route

1. **Day 1: Berlin to Bolzano** Night train via Munich, 11 hours
2. **Days 2 and 3: Dobbiaco** Green meadows, wood stove, strudel
3. **Days 4 to 6: Cortina d'Ampezzo** Day walks from the bus station
4. **Day 7: Misurina** The lake, early, before the cars
5. **Days 8 and 9: Back via Fortezza** One last espresso, night train home
